Event loop
==========
Heap : Area where variables are stored
Stack/CallStack : maintains a Stack of normal functions, calls the functions 1 by 1 from the top

Note: Regular functions can be added to callstack directly, but callback functions cannot be added to callstack directly, they have to wait in queue

MicroTask :  callbacks from promise() , async functions 
Macrotask : callbacks from setTimeOut() , setInterval() 

Event Loop: checks callstack and callback queue
            brings functions from callback ques and gives them to callstack for execution
